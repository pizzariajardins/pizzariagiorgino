## paola
Use case: ads-marketing. Asset: individual square restaurant website food illustration for Giorgino. Create ONE beautiful premium photorealistic styled food artwork, not a collage. A complete round Brazilian pizza Paola, 3/4 elevated viewpoint, all edges inside frame. Thick golden stuffed catupiry crust all around, natural baked dough texture. Toppings strictly: Tomato sauce, mozzarella, visibly chopped ham pieces (never ground), roasted sliced calabresa sausage, separate round dollops of catupiry cream cheese, tiny well-roasted bacon cubes, very finely grated parmesan making a crisp golden lacy top, dried oregano and large black Chilean olives. No basil, no broccoli. Scene: dark warm charcoal stone table, restrained cream/gold editorial Italian restaurant styling, soft warm directional light, appetizing realistic scale and texture, no excessive cheese pull. Pizza occupies about 82% of square. Reserve a clear small dark area in upper right for a real official logo to be composited later via website; do not create any logo or text or invented mark. No lettering whatsoever. Match the same composition, dark palette and camera across the set. This is an illustrative artwork, not claimed as a photo from restaurant.
